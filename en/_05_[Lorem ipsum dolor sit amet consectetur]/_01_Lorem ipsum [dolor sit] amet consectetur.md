# Honk Shoo Mimimi

Honk shoo mimimi. The sound of sleeping. The big elephant honk shooed and the little mouse went mimimi in its tiny bed.

## Sleep Sounds Across Species

Every creature has its own sleep sound. Bears go grrooo. Birds go peep peep. Humans go honk shoo or mimimi depending on size.

- Honk shoo: large creature sleep
- Mimimi: small creature sleep
- Zzz: comic strip sleep
- Grrooo: bear sleep
- Peep peep: bird sleep

<info>
The International Sleep Sound Institute has catalogued over four thousand distinct sleep noises from species across the globe.
</info>
