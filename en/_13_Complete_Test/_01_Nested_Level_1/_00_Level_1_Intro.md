## Mathematical Foundation

The fundamental theorem of calculus states:

$$
\int_a^b f(x) dx = F(b) - F(a)
$$

Where $F$ is the antiderivative of $f$.

## Code Example

```python
def calculate_area(radius):
    """Calculate circle area."""
    import math
    return math.pi * radius ** 2
```
