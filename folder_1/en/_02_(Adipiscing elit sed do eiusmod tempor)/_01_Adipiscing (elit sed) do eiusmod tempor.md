# Mlem Blep Blorp

Mlem mlem mlem. The cat stuck out its tongue just a little bit. Blep blep blep. The dog did a big ol mlem across its nose.

## Tongue Noises and Their Meanings

Animals make tongue noises. These noises have names. Mlem is a lick. Blep is a tongue stick-out. Blorp is a noise of indeterminate origin.

- Mlem: active licking motion
- Blep: passive tongue protrusion
- Blorp: sudden wet noise
- Splorp: exaggerated blorp

<info>
The study of mlem and blep is crucial to understanding animal behavior. Each tongue position tells a story.
</info>

When a cat does a blep, it means they forgot to put their tongue back in their mouth. This is the pinnacle of cuteness science.

A mlem indicates grooming behavior or the consumption of treats.

func MakeNoise(animal string) string {

    }
}
